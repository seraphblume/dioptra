# v0.4.0-rc.1 — experimental prospective release

Release date: 2026-10-23 (America/Mexico_City). The owner explicitly approved an **unvalidated experimental RC**, with post-exam comparison, on this date. This is a research prototype, not an autonomous prescribing tool or a validated medical device.

- Shadow comparator: unchanged `v0.3`, version `0.3.0`.
- v0.2 is retired from new prediction runs, not deleted from historical records. Its frozen source/configuration remain for legacy scoring, shared constants, tests and historical compatibility.

- Lock the RC prediction before entering the final clinician Rx; reveal the comparison only after the clinician result is entered. The score evaluates the entered outcome, not an independently verified ground truth.

## Calibration decision and derivation

The development analysis contained 53 cases / 106 eyes. The exploratory stratified continuous-M fit improved leave-one-case-out MAE by only 0.0027 D, with substantial uncertainty. It did **not** validate this release, its adult-only policy or its capped/rounded outputs. No performance improvement is claimed for the RC. The five review-queue cases have not been assumed erroneous or removed. This development set is not the prospective test cohort.

Residual definition: clinician final spherical equivalent minus AR vector-centre M. The previous analysis provided these aggregate sums:

- All 106 eyes: −47.875 D total; global mean `g = -47.875 / 106`.
- Myopia/emmetropia under 40: 74 eyes, residual sum −38.5 D.
- Myopia/emmetropia age 40+: 30 eyes, residual sum −7.75 D.
- The remaining two eyes occupy sparse hyperopic strata. No hyperopic offsets are fitted for deployment.

For the two supported development strata, apply the existing fitter's shrinkage rule `(sum + 8*g)/(n + 8)`, round the offset to two decimal places, and bound it to ±0.50 D. Configuration retains exact aggregate inputs and uncapped estimates for auditability.

| Application group | RC offset in M | v0.3 prior |
|---|---:|---:|
| Myopia/emmetropia, ages 18–39 | −0.50 D | −0.25 D |
| Myopia/emmetropia, ages 40+ | −0.30 D | −0.25 D |
| All groups, under 18 | −0.25 D retained | −0.25 D |
| Low/higher hyperopia, any age | −0.25 D retained | −0.25 D |

**Important:** the under-40 fitting pool includes children. Restricting application to adults is a conservative release policy, not an independently fitted or validated 18–39-year-old model. The global shrinkage target also includes all development eyes. The sparse groups and pediatric prior carry review flags. Every RC result carries an experimental review flag regardless of the agreement score.

The estimator stays median. Vertex handling, quarter-dioptre output rounding, cylinder/axis rules, ADD estimation, repeatability thresholds, and low-cylinder dropping policy remain unchanged. The ±0.50 D limit bounds the **offset**, not the combined estimator/vertex/rounding change from the selected AR line; existing SE-shift review flags remain in force.

The shared engine factory creates separate configuration closures; it never swaps or mutates v0.3 globals. A 500-case synthetic golden digest, generated from the pre-RC engine at `d0ab3aa`, verifies complete v0.3 outputs excluding timestamps. The frozen v0.2 regression remains enabled.

## Agreement, not clinical confidence

After final Rx entry, `rx-agreement-1` reports:

`round(100 × passed checks / applicable checks)`

For each eye, one check each for sphere within ±0.25 D, cylinder within ±0.25 D, and axis within the existing cylinder-dependent tolerance. If either compared cylinder is zero, the axis check is excluded, not counted as a pass. There are therefore 4–6 checks per case. The display includes the numerator and denominator. Exact differences remain visible in the comparison table; ADD is shown separately and is not part of this percentage.

These axis thresholds are the existing lens-tolerance-derived software rule, not a validated clinical endpoint. The percentage is **not** a probability of correctness, clinical confidence, a quality grade for the examiner, or evidence that either prescription is suitable. A 100% result means all applicable checks were within tolerance, not that both prescriptions are identical. High agreement does not remove RC review flags or replace assessment of acuity, comfort, binocular function and ocular health.

## Previous-Rx duration

When Previous Rx is Yes, an optional field records approximate whole months **wearing the current prescription**, not time since the last examination. Suggested durations speed entry. Blank means unknown; 0 means less than one month; accepted range is 0–600 whole months. No/Unknown excludes all previous-Rx data, including stale duration values.

Duration is stored at `input.previousRx.wornMonths`. The revealed comparison shows duration and final-minus-previous SE for each eye. Neither duration nor the previous prescription changes the RC prediction or agreement score. There is no automatic “older than two years” penalty, presumed adaptation diagnosis, or reduced-acuity explanation.

Wearing a prescription for a long time is not sufficient evidence that an eye is “fixated” on it. Non-tolerance can involve prescription, dispensing, pathology and binocular factors ([primary study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19663923/)). Examination intervals are a separate clinical decision: the [AOA's 2023 guidance](https://www.aoa.org/about-the-aoa/press-room/press-releases/updated-clinical-guideline-reinforces-importance-of-annual-eye-exams-comprehensive-eye-care-with-doctors-of-optometry) recommends annual adult examinations; the app does not infer an interval from prescription age.

## Storage and export

Study CSV columns remain unchanged. The engine-comparison CSV preserves its existing prefix and appends:

- `previous_rx_worn_months`
- `agreement_percent`, `agreement_passed`, `agreement_total`, `agreement_version`
- `entry_revision`

Each new case has primary/shadow and three alternative estimators for **each** engine: eight variants × two eyes. Each variant exports its own offset/evidence, not the comparator's. Agreement is case-level, repeated on both eye rows; do not count those repetitions as independent outcomes. Historical rows leave the new agreement fields blank and preserve original predictions. New case records also retain the full score/check breakdown and RC calibration provenance in browser storage.

No patient-level training rows or clinical notes are included in the repository. Keep the original private exports and analysis as the development audit trail. After release, evaluate complete capped/rounded RC predictions against the paired v0.3 comparator on the new cohort. Predefine cohort closure and analysis rules; do not repeatedly refit this RC while prospective testing continues. Changing calibration requires a new release identifier and cohort.
