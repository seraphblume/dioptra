Algorithm v0 assumptions

v0 is intentionally conservative and transparent. It predicts from pre-refraction data only, quantizes final axis to 5° steps, and uses configurable heuristics rather than machine learning.

The purpose is to collect blinded comparison data. Do not tune the algorithm against individual cases during a shift. Review changes only after a predefined batch of cases.
